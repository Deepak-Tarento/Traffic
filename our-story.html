Our Story

How a small team obsessed with accurate traffic counts grew into a multi-disciplinary data, security and software company.

2018 · The beginning

Founded by Deepmala & Riesth Kumar

Traffic Monster started with a simple conviction: manual, video-based counting done carefully beats rushed estimates. That focus on accuracy became our foundation.

Growing expertise

8+ years in video analysis

Study after study, we refined our counting and QA process — becoming one of India's most rapidly expanding manual traffic data management firms.

Going international

A premier traffic survey company

Our client base grew beyond India, and our process scaled with it — without losing the attention to detail we started with.

Quality certified

ISO 9001:2015

We formalised our quality management, earning ISO 9001:2015 certification and MSME registration.

Three alliances

Traffic, security & software

As clients asked for more, we added dedicated cyber security and software development practices — one team, three specialisations.

Today

Best-rated, still counting

With a 4.9/5 rating and a 50+ strong team, we keep doing what we do best: turning traffic data into decisions our clients trust.

Empowering data. Illuminating futures.

Be part of the next chapter

Whether you have a study to run or a team to join, we'd love to hear from you.
