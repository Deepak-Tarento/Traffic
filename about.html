About Aurion

A company specialising in traffic data count and analysis — with a dedicated team for software development.

Who We Are

Precision data, dependable delivery

Founded in 2018 by Deepmala and Riesth Kumar, Aurion has grown into one of India's most rapidly expanding manual traffic data management firms — now serving clients internationally as a premier traffic survey company.

We specialise in traffic data count and analysis. Our dedicated team meticulously counts traffic data from recorded video, providing accurate insights into traffic patterns for informed decision-making. Structured as two specialised alliances — traffic analysis and software development — we combine domain expertise with engineering discipline.
